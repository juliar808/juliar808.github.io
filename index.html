I’m a research assistant at Epic Lab at Oregon State University, where I work with PhD students studying how AI tools affect the way people think while solving problems. Our current study uses EEG to compare what happens in the brain when someone lets an AI agent write all the code versus using AI as a tool alongside their own work. My role spans the whole research process. I review existing literature to help shape the direction of each study, recruit and schedule participants, and step in to run study sessions when needed. Working in the lab has taught me how research gets done, from the first question to the final paper, and it’s where I get to apply what I learn in my classes to open problems.
